Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.

Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
