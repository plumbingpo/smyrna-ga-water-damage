Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Smyrna

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
