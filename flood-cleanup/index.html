A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. For flood emergencies anywhere in Smyrna, Smyrna Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Smyrna

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
