Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. For emergency water extraction in Smyrna, Smyrna Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
