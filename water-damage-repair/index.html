Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Save spare tile and flooring, plus paint color details, for matching later repairs.
  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.

Signs You Need Water Damage Repair in Smyrna

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Following water from above, part of the ceiling droops, has cracks or crumbles when touched.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.

Why Professional Water Damage Repair Matters

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. A rebuilder must know the path the water took, the harm it caused and if the structure hidden by the wall is actually dry. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. The second skill is matching. If the patch stands out, it reminds you of the damage daily.
